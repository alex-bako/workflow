# Accepted website contract

Build a simple launch page for "Small Tools". The page uses Arial/sans-serif on a
white background, dark text and blue primary controls. Content sits in a centered
column no wider than 720px, with at least 24px horizontal padding on mobile.

Required content: heading "Small Tools", a short description, a primary "Get
started" link that moves to the signup section, and a signup form with a visible
Email label, email input and "Join waitlist" button.

At viewport widths 1280px and 375px all content must fit without horizontal scroll.
Keyboard users must be able to reach and operate the CTA and form. Submitting a
valid email shows "You're on the list." without navigating away. Browser validation
must reject an empty or invalid email. No backend integration is required.
